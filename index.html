A JOURNEY THROUGH ITALY

Italy, one page
at a time.

A little lost, a lot in love, and somewhere between espresso and cobblestone streets.

Start the Journey →

THE ROUTE

Every stop tells a story.

Follow the little dots. They lead to big memories.

01 Milan 02 Venice 03 Florence 04 Bologna 05 Rome 06 Naples 07 Amalfi

DAY 01 · THE BEGINNING

Milan

Where fashion meets history.

My Italian adventure begins in Milan, a city of beautiful architecture, busy streets, stylish cafes and unforgettable first impressions.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Try the famous Risotto alla Milanese.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Camera · Walking shoes · Curiosity

DAY 02 · LOST IN THE CITY

Venice

A city that floats in a dream.

Venice is a maze of tiny streets, quiet bridges and canals. Every turn feels like a discovery, and every moment feels like a postcard.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Try traditional Venetian cicchetti snacks.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Map · Comfortable shoes · An open mind

DAY 03 · ART & WONDER

Florence

Art lives on every street.

Florence feels like walking through an open-air museum. The city is filled with beautiful buildings, art, small shops and wonderful views.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Enjoy a scoop of Italian gelato.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Sketchbook · Camera · Gelato money

DAY 04 · THE FOOD STOP

Bologna

A love letter written with pasta.

Bologna welcomes me with warm colours, long streets and the smell of fresh food. Every meal feels like a special experience.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Taste the famous Tagliatelle al Ragù.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Appetite · Notebook · Extra time

DAY 05 · THROUGH HISTORY

Rome

The past is everywhere.

Rome is a city where history rises from every street. Ancient ruins stand beside cafes, fountains and lively squares.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Try classic Roman pasta like Carbonara.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Water bottle · Hat · Walking shoes

DAY 06 · FULL OF LIFE

Naples

Loud streets, warm hearts.

Naples is colourful and full of life. The streets are filled with conversations, music, scooters and the smell of freshly baked pizza.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Experience authentic Neapolitan pizza.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Camera · Street map · Big appetite

DAY 07 · THE COAST

Amalfi

Where the mountains meet the sea.

Colourful houses sit beside the sea, and the beautiful coastal views make Amalfi the perfect ending to my Italian adventure.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Try lemon desserts inspired by the Amalfi Coast.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Sunglasses · Sunscreen · No plans
