A postcard from Milan

DAY 01 · THE BEGINNING

Milano

Where fashion meets history.

My first Italian ritual: espresso at the counter. I climb above the Duomo’s marble spires, then follow an old orange tram to the Navigli for a slow evening aperitivo.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Taste the creamy Risotto alla Milanese and experience the city's love for slow meals and good conversation.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Camera · Walking shoes · Curiosity

Postcard from Venezia

DAY 02 · LOST IN THE CITY

Venezia

A city that floats in a dream.

No cars, just footsteps and water. I leave the busy lanes for Cannaregio, watch a gondolier glide by and join the locals at a bacaro. Venice is best without a map.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Try cicchetti, small Venetian snacks, paired with the atmosphere of a local café.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Map · Comfortable shoes · An open mind

A postcard from Firenze

DAY 03 · ART & WONDER

Firenze

Art lives on every street.

Under the Duomo’s red dome, art spills into daily life. An artisan shows me her leather workshop. At sunset I cross the Arno, gelato in hand, with nowhere to rush.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Enjoy a scoop of Italian gelato while wandering through the historic streets.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Sketchbook · Camera · Gelato money

Postcard from Bologna

DAY 04 · THE FOOD STOP

Bologna

A love letter written with pasta.

Beneath the red porticoes, I find a little pasta class. Flour on my sleeves, I learn to fold tortellini by hand. Lunch lasts for hours; the conversation lasts even longer.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Discover tagliatelle al ragù and the rich culinary traditions of Bologna.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Appetite · Notebook · Extra time

A postcard from Roma

DAY 05 · THROUGH HISTORY

Roma

The past is everywhere.

Rome is a conversation between centuries. I walk from the Colosseum to a lively market, then spend the evening in Trastevere. The best souvenir is a recipe for carbonara.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Taste classic Roman pasta, from carbonara to cacio e pepe.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Water bottle · Hat · Walking shoes

Postcard from Napoli

DAY 06 · FULL OF LIFE

Napoli

Loud streets, warm hearts.

Naples greets me with scooters, laundry lines and laughter. A pizzaiolo turns dough into dinner in a blazing oven. I eat my margherita folded, standing in the street.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Experience authentic Neapolitan pizza, with its soft crust and simple, unforgettable flavours.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Camera · Street map · Big appetite

A postcard from Amalfi

DAY 07 · THE COAST

Amalfi

Where the mountains meet the sea.

I trade train tracks for coastal paths. Between lemon groves and fishing boats, a family-run trattoria saves me a seat. Lunch tastes of the sea, sunshine and doing less.

🍝 LOCAL BITE

Refresh with a lemon-inspired treat and enjoy the flavours of the coast.

✦ TRAVEL KIT

Sunglasses · Sunscreen · No plans
